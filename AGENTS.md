Working on gwerlas.podman
=========================

Everything a contributor needs is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), which
keeps what is specific to this role and links the
[engineering handbook](https://gitlab.com/yoanncolin/handbook/-/blob/main/README.md)
and the Ansible family's
[reference guide](https://gitlab.com/yoanncolin/ansible/collections/system/-/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md),
with the
[principles](https://gitlab.com/yoanncolin/ansible/collections/system/-/blob/main/docs/principles.md)
it enforces, for the rest. Two handbook pages govern what you *write* rather
than what you change, and they are the ones most easily skipped:

- [Documentation](https://gitlab.com/yoanncolin/handbook/-/blob/main/documentation.md#where-a-rationale-lives):
  which home holds a given reason, and the three deletion tests that decide.
  Run them on what you produced, before calling a change ready.
- [Git](https://gitlab.com/yoanncolin/handbook/-/blob/main/git.md): a change
  arrives with its tests and documentation in the same commit, and an issue is
  referenced from the commit body and nowhere else.

No rule is restated here, on purpose: a second copy of a rule is a rule that
will diverge from the first.
